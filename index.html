01 · the loop

Four steps. No feelings.

  1. 01

    watch

    Every coin that migrates off pump.fun is scanned for liquidity, holder spread and dev wallet.

  2. 02

    score

    A breakout score from volume, price against the migration price, and buyer count. Under 0.70 it skips.

  3. 03

    enter

    Fixed size. Three open positions at most. It never averages down.

  4. 04

    trail

    A 12% trailing stop, a hard stop at −20%, and it scales out at +50% and +100%.
